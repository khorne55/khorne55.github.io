# Building practical systems across hardware, software, and automation

I design and build projects around embedded systems, home automation, programming, and connected devices.

From Arduino prototypes to larger software systems, this site is a place to document ideas, experiments, and engineering work that turns concepts into something usable.

[About me](/about/){: .button }

## What I work on

### Embedded and IoT

I build projects involving microcontrollers, sensors, device communication, and automation workflows. My focus is on making systems reliable, understandable, and useful in real environments.

### Software and engineering

I enjoy working on backend logic, integrations, data flow, and system design. That includes everything from small utilities to larger engineering projects that connect hardware with software.

### Experimental projects

I regularly explore new ideas in computer engineering, including rapid prototyping, homebrew electronics, technical problem-solving, and practical builds that mix code with physical systems.

## Featured areas

- Embedded development with Arduino and microcontrollers.
- Home automation and smart device integration.
- Programming projects and engineering experiments.
- System design, prototyping, and technical documentation.

## Why this site exists

I wanted a simple place to share what I build, what I learn, and how I approach engineering problems.

The goal is not to overcomplicate things. It is to create practical, thoughtful projects and explain them clearly.
