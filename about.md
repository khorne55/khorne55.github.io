## About this site

This website is a personal space for sharing work in computer engineering, embedded systems, automation, and software development.

It brings together technical projects, practical experiments, and lessons learned from building systems that combine hardware and software.

## What I focus on

My main interests include:

- Computer engineering
- Embedded systems
- Arduino and microcontroller projects
- Home automation
- IoT and connected devices
- Programming and backend logic
- Prototyping and technical problem-solving

## Approach

I like projects that are practical, well-structured, and grounded in real use cases.

Whether the work starts with code, electronics, or an automation idea, I care most about reliability, clarity, and building things that are actually useful.

## What you'll find here

On this site, I share:

- Project write-ups
- Engineering notes
- Build logs
- Experiments and prototypes
- Thoughts on tools, systems, and implementation

This is an evolving collection of work and ideas centered on making technology understandable and useful.
